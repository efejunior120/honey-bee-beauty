Our Story • Honey Bee Beauty Hive

A Sanctuary Dedicated to Healthy, Confident Skin.

"True beauty isn't about covering up imperfections — it is about awakening your skin's natural glow so you feel radiantly confident without makeup."

Founded in Effurun, Warri, Delta State, Honey Bee Beauty Hive (Spa & Facials) was created out of a deep passion for clinical skincare results blended with the comforting warmth of a luxury retreat.

Recognizing the impact of Delta's tropical weather, humidity, and pollution on skin pores, our studio specialized in deep restorative steaming, pore decongestion, and gentle nourishing treatments using pure honey botanicals.
